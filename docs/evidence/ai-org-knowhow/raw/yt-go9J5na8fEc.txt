## Description

```
Turn Claude from a chat window into something closer to a chief of staff: a tool that knows your role, your brand, your stakeholders and your standards, and produces real deliverables you can send the same day.

In this 60-minute session I walk professionals through the exact five-layer setup I use in Claude Cowork, plus live demos of board packs, consultant-grade reports, HTML proposals and personal dashboards. No coding. No design background. Just an imagination and a conversation.

I'm Terez Rijkenberg, an executive coach who has spent eight years working with C-suite leaders on productivity, confidence and building self-directed teams.

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

The five layers that make AI actually useful for professional work:

1. Context. Why the setup matters more than the prompt, and how to give Claude your role, brand, voice, objectives and stakeholder map once instead of re-explaining every session.
2. Projects. Why one project per business beats a project per task, and how memory builds over time.
3. Skills. What a skill is, how to build one, and the rule I use: never do the same thing twice.
4. Automation. Turning weekly and monthly work into scheduled tasks.
5. Dashboards. Making the invisible visible, for work and for life.

DEMOS IN THIS SESSION

- A branded board pack built from spreadsheet data
- A consultant-grade strategy report, researched and written in about 15 minutes
- An interactive HTML proposal that replaced my PDF and email version
- A weekday morning brief built from calendar and email connectors
- A transcript scrubber that strips client-identifying data before analysis
- A coaching question bank a client uses before every one-to-one
- A family dashboard, because summer holidays are their own project

ALSO COVERED

- Privacy and data settings you should check today, including the two toggles to turn off and why the thumbs-up button matters
- The difference between context and skills, and when to use each
- Working across multiple brands or clients without cross contamination
- Whether any of this works in Copilot or ChatGPT (the principles do)
```
