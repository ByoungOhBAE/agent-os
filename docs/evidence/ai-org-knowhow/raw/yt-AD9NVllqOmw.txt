## Description

```
Module 5 begins: one agent becomes a manager of specialists. Instead of asking a single agent to plan AND research AND write AND fact-check — where it ends up mediocre at all four — an orchestrator decomposes the goal and delegates each piece to a focused worker.

You'll learn why a team beats a single mega-agent (focus, separate tools, parallelism), why a worker is itself a full agent (its own prompt, tools and loop — delegation is recursive), the orchestrator's decompose→dispatch→collect→synthesize cycle, how to route subtasks, and the pitfalls of over-delegating.
```
